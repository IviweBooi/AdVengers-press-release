The Problem

~95% of Takealot sellers don't know if their ads are working and nobody is telling them.

Thousands of unmanaged sellers on the Takealot marketplace spend money on advertising with no guidance on whether it is working. They see numbers: impressions, ROAS, ACoS, but don't know what those numbers mean for their specific business and category.

They pause campaigns. They raise budgets on campaigns that are losing money. They blame the ad when the real problem is price, stock, or a lost Buy Box. They miss sales not because the product is wrong, but because nobody told them what to fix first.

The Solution
Not
dashboards.
Advice.

Ads Advisor reads each seller's real campaign data — spend history, conversion rates, ROAS, product quality signals — and tells them exactly what to do next and why. In plain language. Specific to their situation.

01  —  Read
Real data

Pulls live campaign metrics, monthly spend history, product listing signals, and category benchmarks for every seller automatically.

02  —  Interpret
In context

Compares the seller's numbers against their specific category averages — not generic benchmarks — to surface what's actually wrong.

03  —  Advise
One next step

Tells the seller one specific thing to change and why — not a list of optimisations but a single actionable recommendation.

How it works

A seller logs in.
Their advisor already knows their story.

Before the seller types anything, Ads Advisor has already loaded their campaign history, product data, and spending patterns. The conversation starts informed — not from zero.

01
Login as your seller account Search by name or seller ID. One click to enter. No password required in the prototype.
02
Advisor loads your data Campaign performance, product quality signals, spend history, and category benchmarks are all read automatically.
03
Ask anything or explore your pages Chat with the advisor directly, or browse the Campaigns, Products, and Reports views — all populated with your real data.
04
Get a specific action, not a summary The advisor tells you what to change in the Seller Portal and why — never "optimise your campaigns."
